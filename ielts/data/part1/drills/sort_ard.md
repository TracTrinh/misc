type: sort

title: Xếp tầng phản xạ A-R-D
intro: Gán mỗi mảnh câu vào đúng vị trí của công thức Answer - Reason - Detail.
buckets: ANSWER | REASON | DETAIL

---

sentence: Yes, I absolutely love listening to music.
answer: ANSWER

---

sentence: Because it really helps me unwind after a long, stressful day at school.
answer: REASON

---

sentence: I usually put on some lo-fi tracks on Spotify every evening around 9 PM.
answer: DETAIL

---

sentence: To be honest, I rarely cook at home these days.
answer: ANSWER

---

sentence: Mostly because my schedule is completely packed and I do not have much free time.
answer: REASON

---

sentence: I usually just order a quick box of chicken rice from a food app near my office.
answer: DETAIL

---

sentence: Actually, I prefer traveling by bus rather than riding a motorbike.
answer: ANSWER

---

sentence: It is a lot safer, and I can avoid the terrible traffic dust during rush hour.
answer: REASON

---

sentence: I catch the number 14 bus every morning at 7 AM right in front of my apartment building.
answer: DETAIL
