intro: Đọc câu hỏi Part 1 và chọn đúng dạng để áp dụng phản xạ phù hợp.
types: Like/Dislike | Frequency | Past/Now | Preference | Description | Future/Would

---

question: How often do you go to the cinema to watch movies?
answer: Frequency

---

question: Do you prefer studying in the morning or in the evening?
answer: Preference

---

question: What does your hometown look like?
answer: Description

---

question: Did you enjoy your time at primary school when you were a child?
answer: Past/Now

---

question: Would you like to live in another country in the future?
answer: Future/Would

---

question: Is there anything you dislike about your current job or studies?
answer: Like/Dislike

---

question: Do you usually spend your weekends with your family or friends?
answer: Preference

---

question: How have your hobbies changed since you were younger?
answer: Past/Now

---

question: What do you usually do when you hang out with your friends?
answer: Description

---

question: Do you think you will continue doing your current hobby for a long time?
answer: Future/Would
