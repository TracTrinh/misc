title: Cứu câu cụt Part 1
intro: Viết lại thành câu Part 1 tốt theo Answer + Reason + Detail thật, rồi so với gợi ý.
---
prompt: Do you like reading? — "Yes, I do."
hint: Đọc gì? Khi nào? Cảm giác ra sao?
model: Yes, I do, but I mostly read short things. I usually read articles on my phone at night because they are easy to finish.
---
prompt: Do you often cook? — "Sometimes."
hint: Món gì? Lúc nào? Vì sao không thường xuyên?
model: Sometimes, especially on weekends. I cook simple dishes like fried rice because I am usually too busy on weekdays.
---
prompt: Do you prefer texting or calling? — "Texting."
hint: Vì sao? Dùng với ai? Tình huống nào?
model: I prefer texting because it gives me time to think. I usually text my friends during the day and only call when something is urgent.
---
prompt: Do you like your room? — "Yes, it is nice."
hint: Chi tiết thật trong phòng? Vì sao thích?
model: Yes, I like it because it feels quiet and private. My favorite part is the small desk near the window where I study.
---
prompt: Did you play sports as a child? — "No, not really."
hint: Hồi nhỏ làm gì thay thế? Vì sao?
model: Not really. I was quite shy and preferred staying indoors, so I spent more time drawing or watching cartoons.
---
prompt: Would you like to travel more? — "Yes, of course."
hint: Đi đâu? Vì sao? Khi nào?
model: Yes, I would. I would like to visit Japan one day because I am interested in the food and old temples there.
---
prompt: Do you like shopping? — "Not much."
hint: Loại shopping nào? Vì sao?
model: Not much. I only enjoy shopping when I need something specific because walking around stores for hours makes me tired.
---
prompt: What do you do after work? — "I rest."
hint: Nghỉ bằng cách nào? Chi tiết thời gian?
model: I usually rest at home after work. I make a simple dinner and watch a short video before going to bed.
---
prompt: Do you like flowers? — "Yes."
hint: Loại hoa nào? Gặp ở đâu?
model: Yes, I do. I especially like sunflowers because they look bright, and I sometimes see them at a small flower shop near my office.
---
prompt: Do you use a computer often? — "Every day."
hint: Làm gì trên máy tính? Vì sao?
model: Every day. I use my laptop for work and study, so I normally spend several hours typing documents or joining online meetings.
