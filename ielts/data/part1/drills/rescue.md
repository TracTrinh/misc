title: Cứu câu cụt
intro: Câu dưới quá cụt hoặc quá to. Viết lại thành câu Part 1 tốt (Answer + Reason + Detail thật), rồi so với gợi ý.
---
prompt: Do you like reading? — "Yes, I do."
hint: Đọc loại gì? Khi nào? Cảm giác ra sao?
model: Yes, but I don't read every day. I usually read short articles at night when I have quiet time. They give me something to think about.
---
prompt: Do you enjoy cooking? — "No, I don't."
hint: Vì sao? Khi nào mới nấu? Món đơn giản nào?
model: Not really. I find cooking a bit tiring. I only cook simple meals like noodles or eggs when I have to.
---
prompt: Do you like coffee? — "Coffee is important because it helps people be productive."
hint: Kéo Big Idea về Small Truth: mình uống khi nào, mấy ly?
model: Yes, but only in the morning. I usually drink one cup before work because it helps me feel more awake.
