title: Cứu câu cụt Part 1 Set 2
intro: Viết lại câu trả lời Part 1 theo Answer + Reason + Detail thật, rồi so với gợi ý.
---
prompt: Do you like rainy weather? — "Yes, I do."
hint: Rainy when? What do you usually do?
model: Yes, I do, especially when I can stay at home. Rain makes the day feel calmer, so I usually make a hot drink and do some reading.
---
prompt: How often do you use public transport? — "Sometimes."
hint: Which transport? For what purpose?
model: Not very often. I usually ride my motorbike, but I take the bus when I need to go somewhere far across the city.
---
prompt: Do you like museums? — "Not really."
hint: Why not? Any exception?
model: Not really, because I get tired if there is too much information. But I do enjoy small museums with clear displays and a quiet atmosphere.
---
prompt: Do you buy clothes online? — "Yes."
hint: What kind? Why online?
model: Yes, but only simple items like T-shirts. I don't like guessing sizes, so I usually buy from the same shop again.
---
prompt: Do you like cafés? — "Yes, very much."
hint: What kind of café? When do you go?
model: Yes, I like quiet cafés. I sometimes go there in the afternoon to read or finish some small tasks on my laptop.
---
prompt: Do you follow the news? — "A little."
hint: Where do you read it? What kind of news?
model: A little. I usually check headlines on my phone in the morning, mostly local news and technology news.
---
prompt: Would you like to learn a new skill? — "Yes, cooking."
hint: What food? Why useful?
model: Yes, I'd like to cook better. I can make simple meals now, but I want to learn a few healthy dishes for busy weekdays.
---
prompt: Do you like giving gifts? — "Yes."
hint: Who for? What type?
model: Yes, especially for close friends. I prefer small useful gifts, like a book, a pen, or something they can use every day.
---
prompt: How often do you visit parks? — "Rarely."
hint: Why rarely? When would you go?
model: Rarely, because there isn't a good park very close to my home. I only go when I want to take a long walk on the weekend.
---
prompt: Do you like animals? — "Yes, dogs."
hint: Why dogs? Any real detail?
model: Yes, I like dogs because they seem friendly and expressive. My neighbour has a small dog that always runs to the gate when people pass by.
