title: Thêm Human Detail
intro: Dùng 5 cửa (When, Where, Who with, What kind, Feeling) để nới câu quá cụt thành một Detail có đời sống thật.
---
prompt: I like music.
hint: Nghe khi nào? Nghe loại gì?
model: I usually listen to slow songs before sleeping. It helps me calm down after a long day.
---
prompt: I like my room.
hint: Trong phòng có gì làm em thích?
model: It feels very cozy. I have a small desk by the window where I often read books.
---
prompt: I don't often meet my friends.
hint: Vì sao? Khi gặp thì thường làm gì?
model: Everyone is quite busy with work, but when we do meet, we usually go to a quiet cafe to catch up.
---
prompt: I like shopping sometimes.
hint: Mua gì? Online hay ở cửa hàng?
model: I usually shop online for clothes or small household items because it saves a lot of time.
---
prompt: I enjoy weekends.
hint: Thích khoảnh khắc nào nhất?
model: I love waking up late on Sunday mornings and having a slow breakfast without rushing.
