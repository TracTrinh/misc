title: Sinh tồn với topic lạ (A-C-D)
intro: Gặp topic nhỏ/lạ (mirrors, coins, handwriting, noise…) đừng cố thành chuyên gia. Mở nhẹ (Admit) → nối về đời sống (Connect) → thêm một chi tiết (Detail). Viết thử rồi so gợi ý.

---

prompt: Do you like mirrors?
hint: A: không nghĩ nhiều · C: mình dùng khi nào · D: một chi tiết thật
model: I don't think about mirrors much, but I use one every day before going out. It helps me check if I look presentable, so I guess it's quite useful.

---

prompt: Do you often use coins?
hint: A-C-D: thừa nhận nhẹ → còn dùng không → khi nào
model: I don't think about coins much, but I still use them sometimes for small payments, like buying water or paying for parking.

---

prompt: Do you like maps?
hint: Nối về bản đồ số mình hay dùng
model: I wouldn't say I'm interested in maps, but I use digital maps quite often, especially when I go somewhere new and I'm afraid of getting lost.

---

prompt: How do you feel about noise?
hint: Có hai mặt — dùng "It depends"
model: I guess it depends on the situation. Soft background noise is fine, and I can even study with it, but loud traffic really makes me feel tired.

---

prompt: Do you still write by hand?
hint: Past/Now: used to… but now…
model: I used to write by hand a lot at school, but now I mostly type on my phone or laptop. I only write by hand when I need to jot down a quick note.

---

prompt: Do you like perfume?
hint: Không mê nhưng vẫn có liên hệ nhẹ
model: It's not something I pay much attention to, but I do like light, fresh smells. I don't really use strong perfume because it can give me a slight headache.
