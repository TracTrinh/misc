---
question: Describe a person who has influenced you.<br>- Who this person is<br>- How you know this person<br>- What this person has done<br>And explain how they influenced you.
---
question: Describe a place in your hometown that you like.<br>- Where it is<br>- What it looks like<br>- What people do there<br>And explain why you like it.
---
question: Describe a useful thing you own.<br>- What it is<br>- How you got it<br>- How often you use it<br>And explain why it is useful.
---
question: Describe a time when you learned something new.<br>- What you learned<br>- When you learned it<br>- How you learned it<br>And explain why it was useful.
---
question: Describe a journey you enjoyed.<br>- Where you went<br>- Who you went with<br>- What happened during the journey<br>And explain why you enjoyed it.
---
question: Describe a difficult decision you made.<br>- What the decision was<br>- When you made it<br>- Why it was difficult<br>And explain what happened afterwards.
---
question: Describe an activity you do with other people.<br>- What the activity is<br>- Who you do it with<br>- Where you do it<br>And explain why you enjoy it.
---
question: Describe a book or film that made you think.<br>- What it was<br>- When you read or watched it<br>- What it was about<br>And explain why it made you think.
---
question: Describe a time when someone gave you good advice.<br>- Who gave you the advice<br>- What the advice was<br>- What situation you were in<br>And explain why the advice was helpful.
---
question: Describe a public place you think should be improved.<br>- What place it is<br>- Where it is<br>- How people use it<br>And explain how it should be improved.
