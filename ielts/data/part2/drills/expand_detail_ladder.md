title: Detail Ladder (Thang chi tiết)
intro: Nâng cấp chi tiết từ General -> Situation -> Inner feeling -> Human detail.
---
prompt: I was tired.
hint: Đưa vào Situation -> Inner feeling -> Human detail (vd: mắt nặng, ngáp)
model: I had been working on my laptop for 8 hours straight. I felt completely exhausted, and my eyes were so heavy that I could barely keep them open.
---
prompt: The place was crowded.
hint: Hoàn cảnh -> Cảm giác -> Chi tiết con người
model: It was the weekend market, and there were people everywhere. It was so packed that we were constantly bumping into others and had to shout just to hear each other.
