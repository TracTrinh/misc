title: Value Line (Câu đóng dư âm)
intro: Đừng kết bằng "I learned a lot". Kết bằng một câu nuance có chiều sâu.
---
prompt: It taught me that hard work is good.
hint: Kết nối hard work với kết quả bất ngờ hoặc sự kiên nhẫn
model: It taught me that success isn't always about being the smartest person in the room; sometimes it's just about having the patience to not give up when things get hard.
---
prompt: I learned that friends are important.
hint: Câu đóng sâu sắc hơn về tình bạn
model: It made me realize that true friendship isn't about being together all the time, but about knowing someone is there for you when you really need them.
