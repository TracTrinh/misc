intro: Mở rộng câu mỏng thành một mẩu chuyện Part 2 có TIME, HOW, WHY, VALUE.
---
prompt: Describe a useful thing you bought online. — "I bought a desk lamp online. It was useful."
hint: Khi nào mua? Vì sao cần? Mua thế nào? Nó giúp gì?
model: I bought a desk lamp online about six months ago, when I started studying in the evening more often. My room light was too weak, so I checked reviews and chose a small lamp with adjustable brightness. It was useful immediately because I could read without hurting my eyes. The experience taught me that a small, practical purchase can improve a daily routine a lot.
---
prompt: Describe a neighbour you know. — "My neighbour is friendly."
hint: Gặp khi nào? Người đó làm gì? Vì sao đáng nhớ?
model: One neighbour I remember is an elderly woman who lives near my house. I first noticed her because she always greeted people at the gate in the morning. Once, when a delivery driver couldn't find my address, she helped him call me and waited outside for a few minutes. It was a small act, but it made the neighbourhood feel warmer and safer.
---
prompt: Describe a time you used public transport. — "I took a bus. It was okay."
hint: Đi đâu? Vì sao đi bus? Chuyện gì xảy ra?
model: I took a bus across the city last month because my motorbike was being repaired. At first, I was worried about missing the stop, so I followed the route on my phone the whole time. The bus was crowded, but it was cheaper and calmer than I expected. After that trip, I felt more confident using public transport when I really need to.
---
prompt: Describe a celebration you enjoyed. — "I enjoyed a family party."
hint: Dịp gì? Ai ở đó? Có chi tiết nào thật?
model: I enjoyed a small family celebration for my grandmother's birthday. It was held at home, so the atmosphere was simple and relaxed. Everyone brought one dish, and we spent most of the evening talking rather than taking photos. I liked it because it reminded me that a meaningful celebration does not need to be expensive or formal.
---
prompt: Describe a skill you learned. — "I learned cooking."
hint: Học lúc nào? Học món gì? Gặp khó gì?
model: I learned to cook a few basic meals when I started spending more time at home. At first, I could only make instant noodles, so I watched short videos and practised simple dishes like fried rice and soup. Some attempts tasted quite plain, but gradually I became more confident. The skill is valuable because it gives me more control over my health and schedule.
---
prompt: Describe an advertisement you remember. — "I remember a language app ad."
hint: Quảng cáo trông thế nào? Vì sao nhớ?
model: I remember an advertisement for a language learning app that showed a busy office worker learning words during short breaks. The ad was not flashy, but the situation felt realistic. I remembered it because it made language learning look manageable, even for people with limited time. It also reminded me that progress can come from small daily habits.
---
prompt: Describe a park you visited. — "I went to a park and walked around."
hint: Khi nào? Cảnh vật? Vì sao thích?
model: I visited a public park early one Saturday morning after a stressful week. The weather was cool, and there were people jogging, children playing, and older people doing light exercise. I walked slowly around the lake and sat on a bench for a while. It was memorable because it helped me clear my head without doing anything special.
---
prompt: Describe a time you worked in a team. — "We did a group project."
hint: Dự án gì? Vấn đề gì? Vai trò của bạn? Bài học?
model: I worked in a team during a class presentation at university. At first, we were disorganised because everyone assumed someone else would lead. I suggested dividing the task into research, slides, and speaking parts, and after that we worked more smoothly. The experience taught me that teamwork becomes easier when responsibilities are clear.
