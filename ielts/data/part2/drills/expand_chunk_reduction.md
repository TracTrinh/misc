type: expand
title: Chunk Reduction
intro: Rút gọn các câu dẫn mẫu (chunk) để bớt lộ công thức. VD: "What made it special was that..." -> "What stayed with me was...".
---
prompt: What made this experience special was that my dad came to see me.
hint: Rút gọn tự nhiên hơn (What I remember most / The best part)
model: I think the best part was that my dad actually took a day off work just to come and see me.
---
prompt: This happened when I was in my first year of university.
hint: Rút gọn (Back in... / It was around...)
model: It was around my freshman year of university, a time when I was still trying to figure things out.
