type: expand
title: Soft Contrast
intro: Chuyển hướng nhẹ trong tư duy để câu nói tự nhiên hơn: "I thought..., but..." / "I used to think..., but now...".
---
prompt: The trip was really fun.
hint: Ban đầu nghĩ gì -> Thực tế ra sao
model: To be honest, I thought the trip would be quite boring because it was just a small town, but it actually turned out to be one of the most relaxing holidays I've ever had.
---
prompt: I like reading books now.
hint: Ngày xưa thế nào -> Bây giờ ra sao
model: I used to think that reading was a waste of time and only for studying, but now I realize it's a great way to escape from daily stress.
