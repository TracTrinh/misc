title: Nới bài — Zoom In
intro: Câu quá mỏng. Dùng Zoom In (thời điểm, trạng thái của mình, không khí, cảm giác đầu tiên) để nới ra. Viết thử rồi so với gợi ý.
---
prompt: This happened last year during my final exams.
hint: Lúc đó cuộc sống ra sao? Mình đang trong trạng thái nào?
model: This happened last year during my final exams, which was already a very stressful time for me. At that time, I had several subjects to prepare for, but instead of focusing, I kept wasting time on my phone.
---
prompt: I got lost.
hint: Khi nào? Tại sao lạc? Cảm giác thế nào?
model: I got lost when I was trying to find a new coffee shop for a meeting. My phone battery had just died, and I started to panic because I was already running late.
---
prompt: I was nervous.
hint: Nervous ở đâu? Cơ thể phản ứng thế nào?
model: I was incredibly nervous. My hands were shaking and my mind went completely blank just moments before I had to step onto the stage.
---
prompt: I was happy.
hint: Cơ thể phản ứng sao? Không khí thế nào?
model: I was absolutely thrilled. I remember jumping up and down and calling my mom immediately to tell her the good news.
