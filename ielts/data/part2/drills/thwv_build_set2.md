layers: TIME | HOW | WHY | VALUE

question: Describe a useful item you bought online.<br>- What it was<br>- When you bought it<br>- How you chose it<br>And explain why it was useful.
model_TIME: I bought it a few months ago when I began studying more at night.
model_HOW: I compared reviews online and chose a small desk lamp with adjustable brightness.
model_WHY: It helped because my room light was too weak for comfortable reading.
model_VALUE: It showed me that a small practical item can improve a daily habit.
---
question: Describe a friendly neighbour.<br>- Who this person is<br>- How you know them<br>- What they did<br>And explain why you remember them.
model_TIME: I noticed this neighbour after moving into my current area.
model_HOW: She often greeted people and once helped a delivery driver find my house.
model_WHY: The act was small, but it made me feel welcome in the neighbourhood.
model_VALUE: It reminded me that kindness in daily life does not need to be dramatic.
---
question: Describe a celebration you enjoyed.<br>- What it was<br>- Who was there<br>- What happened<br>And explain why you enjoyed it.
model_TIME: It was my grandmother's birthday celebration at home last year.
model_HOW: Each family member brought some food, and we spent the evening talking.
model_WHY: I enjoyed it because it felt relaxed and genuine, not showy.
model_VALUE: It taught me that simple celebrations can be more meaningful than expensive ones.
---
question: Describe a time you used public transport.<br>- Where you went<br>- Why you used it<br>- What happened<br>And explain how you felt about it.
model_TIME: This happened last month when my motorbike was being repaired.
model_HOW: I took a bus across the city and followed the route on my phone.
model_WHY: I felt nervous at first, but the trip was clearer than I expected.
model_VALUE: It made me more open to using public transport when it is practical.

question: Describe a skill you learned recently.<br>- What the skill was<br>- How you learned it<br>- What was difficult<br>And explain why it is useful.
model_TIME: I started learning basic cooking during a period when I stayed home more.
model_HOW: I watched short videos and practised simple dishes like soup and fried rice.
model_WHY: It was difficult at first because I did not know how to balance flavours.
model_VALUE: Now it helps me save money and take better care of myself.

model_TIME: I saw it online while watching short videos after work.
model_HOW: It advertised a language app through a simple story about an office worker.
model_WHY: I remembered it because the situation felt realistic rather than exaggerated.
model_VALUE: It made me think that learning can fit into small gaps in the day.

model_TIME: I first went there on a quiet weekend morning.
model_HOW: I walked around the lake and watched people jogging and exercising.
model_WHY: The place felt calm and gave me space to think.
model_VALUE: It reminded me that public spaces are important for everyday mental rest.

question: Describe a time you worked well in a team.<br>- What the task was<br>- Who you worked with<br>- What you did<br>And explain why the teamwork was successful.
model_TIME: It happened during a university presentation project.
model_HOW: We divided the work into research, slides, and speaking practice.
model_WHY: The teamwork improved because everyone knew exactly what to do.
model_VALUE: I learned that clear roles are more useful than vague enthusiasm.
