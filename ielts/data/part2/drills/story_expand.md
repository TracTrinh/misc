title: Mở rộng câu chuyện Part 2
intro: Biến ý mỏng thành đoạn kể chuyện ngắn theo T-H-W-V, rồi so với gợi ý.
---
prompt: Describe a gift you received. — "I got a pen from my friend."
hint: Khi nào? Bạn dùng nó thế nào? Vì sao nhớ?
model: I received a pen from my best friend when I started university. It looked simple, but I used it to take notes in my first English class. I remember it because it made me feel that someone believed in me at a nervous time.
---
prompt: Describe a place you like. — "I like a park near my house."
hint: Bối cảnh lần đến gần nhất? Bạn làm gì? Cảm xúc?
model: There is a small park near my house, and I often go there after work. Last month, I went there alone, walked slowly around the lake, and watched older people doing exercise. It is not a famous place, but it gives me a feeling of space and calm.
---
prompt: Describe a person you admire. — "I admire my mother."
hint: Một việc cụ thể mẹ làm? Bạn học gì?
model: I admire my mother because she stays calm even when life is busy. When I was preparing for exams, she quietly made dinner for me every evening and never made me feel guilty. That taught me that real support is often practical, not dramatic.
---
prompt: Describe a skill you learned. — "I learned cooking."
hint: Khi nào học? Ai chỉ? Món đầu tiên?
model: I learned basic cooking when I moved away from home for university. At first, I called my sister almost every night to ask simple questions. The first dish I made was fried rice, and although it was too salty, I felt proud because I could take care of myself.
---
prompt: Describe a time you helped someone. — "I helped my friend study English."
hint: Bạn giúp thế nào? Kết quả ra sao?
model: A few months ago, my friend asked me to help him prepare for a speaking test. We met twice a week, practised simple answers, and recorded his voice. He became more confident, and I realised that teaching someone also makes you understand the skill more clearly.
---
prompt: Describe a trip you enjoyed. — "I went to the beach."
hint: Đi với ai? Hoạt động cụ thể? Vì sao đáng nhớ?
model: Last summer, I went to the beach with my family for two days. We woke up early, walked along the shore, and had seafood at a small restaurant. I enjoyed it because everyone put away their phones for a while and simply talked.
---
prompt: Describe a book you liked. — "I liked a book about habits."
hint: Đọc lúc nào? Ý nào nhớ nhất? Ảnh hưởng?
model: I read a book about habits when I was trying to manage my time better. One idea I still remember is to start with a very small action instead of waiting for motivation. It helped me build a short evening reading routine.
---
prompt: Describe a time you were proud. — "I passed an exam."
hint: Chuẩn bị ra sao? Cảm xúc lúc nhận kết quả?
model: I felt proud when I passed an English exam after several months of practice. I studied every evening, even when I was tired, and often recorded my speaking answers. When I saw the result, I felt relieved more than excited because the effort had finally paid off.
