title: One Story, Many Cue Cards
intro: Dùng cùng một câu chuyện (ví dụ: Teamwork làm dự án trễ hạn) nhưng xoay góc nhấn để trả lời các đề khác nhau.
layers: GÓC NHẤN | TIME | HOW | VALUE
---
question: Describe a time you worked in a team.
model_GÓC NHẤN: Nhấn vào sự hợp tác và phân công.
model_TIME: This happened during my final year when we had a group presentation.
model_HOW: At first, we struggled because everyone had different ideas. But then we sat down, divided the tasks based on strengths, and worked together.
model_VALUE: It taught me that good communication is the key to successful teamwork.
---
question: Describe a stressful experience.
model_GÓC NHẤN: Nhấn vào áp lực thời gian và cảm xúc.
model_TIME: This happened during my final year when we had a tight deadline for a group presentation.
model_HOW: Our laptop crashed just one day before the deadline. We panicked at first, but we stayed up all night to redo the slides.
model_VALUE: It taught me how to stay calm and manage pressure when things go wrong.
