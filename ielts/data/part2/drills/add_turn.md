title: Nới bài — Add a Turn
intro: Câu chuyện cần có khúc ngoặt. Nới tầng HOW bằng công thức: beginning + turning point + result.
---
prompt: I went to the store and bought a gift.
hint: At first... Then/However... In the end...
model: At first, I didn't know what to buy, so I walked around the store for almost an hour. However, I eventually found a beautiful notebook, and in the end, my friend absolutely loved it.
---
prompt: We had a project. We finished it late.
hint: Khởi đầu thế nào -> Khó khăn gì -> Kết quả
model: When we started the project, everything seemed fine. But a few days before the deadline, our laptop crashed. We had to stay up all night to finish it just in time.
---
prompt: I tried to learn how to cook, and I made a good meal.
hint: Ban đầu -> Ngoặt -> Kết quả
model: At first, I was completely terrible at it and even burned some eggs. However, after watching a few videos online, I finally managed to cook a decent bowl of fried rice.
