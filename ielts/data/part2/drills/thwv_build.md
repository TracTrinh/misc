question: Describe a person who encouraged you.<br>- Who this person is<br>- What they encouraged you to do<br>- How they encouraged you<br>And explain why their encouragement mattered.
model_TIME: This happened during my final year of school when I was worried about exams.
model_HOW: My English teacher noticed I was quiet and asked me to practise speaking after class.
model_WHY: Her encouragement mattered because I felt seen at a time when I doubted myself.
model_VALUE: I learned that confidence often begins when one person takes your effort seriously.

model_TIME: I usually go there on weekend mornings when the city is still quiet.
model_HOW: I walk around the lake, sit on a bench, and sometimes read for half an hour.
model_WHY: It helps me relax because the space feels open and simple.
model_VALUE: I learned that rest does not have to be expensive or complicated.

model_WHY: It is useful because it stops me from keeping everything in my head.
model_VALUE: I learned that good tools are helpful only when they support simple habits.

model_VALUE: It taught me to stay calm first and look for the next useful step.
