question: Describe a useful item you bought online.<br>- What it was<br>- When you bought it<br>- How you chose it<br>And explain why it was useful.
---

---
question: Describe a celebration you enjoyed.<br>- What it was<br>- Who was there<br>- What happened<br>And explain why you enjoyed it.

question: Describe a time you used public transport.<br>- Where you went<br>- Why you used it<br>- What happened<br>And explain how you felt about it.
---
question: Describe a skill you learned recently.<br>- What the skill was<br>- How you learned it<br>- What was difficult<br>And explain why it is useful.

---

---
