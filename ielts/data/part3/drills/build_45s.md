model_POSITION: Yes, I strongly believe so, although the format might change.
model_REASON: The main reason is that reading provides a deep level of imagination and focus that short videos simply cannot offer.
model_REALITY: For instance, while many people scroll through social media for quick news, book clubs and e-readers are still incredibly popular among those who want to disconnect from fast-paced media.

question: Should people be encouraged to use public transport more?
model_POSITION: Absolutely, it is one of the most effective ways to protect the environment.
model_REASON: This is mainly because fewer private vehicles on the road mean less exhaust fumes and better air quality for everyone.
model_REALITY: In real life, however, many people still prefer driving because public transport systems in some cities are not reliable or comfortable enough.
model_VALUE: Therefore, the goal is not just to tell people to take the bus, but for the government to invest in making public transport a better option than driving.
