prompt: Should public transport be cheaper? — "Yes, because it helps people."
hint: Giúp ai? Vì sao? Thực tế thành phố? Chốt cân bằng.
model: Yes, in many cities it should be more affordable. If buses or trains are too expensive, low-income workers may have no good alternative to motorbikes or old private vehicles. In reality, price alone is not enough; the service also has to be reliable and safe. So cheaper transport should be part of a wider plan, not a single solution.
---
prompt: Do advertisements influence children? — "Yes, they make children buy things."
hint: Cơ chế ảnh hưởng? Ví dụ xã hội? Không cực đoan.
model: Yes, advertisements can influence children because they are still learning how to judge information. Bright colours, catchy music, and cartoon characters can make a product look more necessary than it really is. This is especially clear with snacks, toys, and games. However, the answer is not to remove all advertising, but to teach children and parents how to respond to it critically.
---
prompt: Are museums still useful? — "Yes, they teach history."
hint: Tại sao khác sách/internet? Thực tế bảo tàng?
model: Yes, museums are still useful because they make history feel concrete. Seeing an old tool, a traditional costume, or a reconstructed room can create a stronger impression than reading a short text online. The problem is that some museums present information in a dry way. To stay relevant, they need clearer storytelling and more interactive displays.
---
prompt: Why do people buy unnecessary things online? — "Because it is easy."
hint: Nhanh, khuyến mãi, ít suy nghĩ, chốt tự kiểm soát.
model: People often buy unnecessary things online because the process is almost too easy. A discount, a countdown timer, and one-click payment can push people to decide before they really think. In daily life, this happens most during big sales or late-night browsing. The deeper issue is not technology itself, but the lack of a pause between wanting something and buying it.
---
prompt: Will printed newspapers disappear? — "Yes, because people use phones."
hint: Dự đoán cân bằng, nhóm tuổi, vai trò còn lại.
model: Printed newspapers will probably become less common, but I don't think they will disappear completely in every place. Phones are faster and more convenient, especially for younger readers who want instant updates. However, some older readers still trust printed newspapers, and some cafés, libraries, or offices may keep them. So the format may shrink, but the need for reliable news will remain.
---
prompt: How can schools teach teamwork? — "They can give group projects."
hint: Không chỉ giao việc nhóm, cần vai trò và đánh giá.
model: Schools can teach teamwork by designing group projects more carefully. Simply putting students into groups does not guarantee cooperation; some students may do all the work while others stay passive. Teachers can assign roles, set checkpoints, and include peer feedback. This teaches students that teamwork is a skill with structure, not just a friendly activity.
---
prompt: Are public parks important in cities? — "Yes, people can relax there."
hint: Không gian đô thị, sức khỏe, công bằng xã hội.
model: Yes, public parks are important because they give people a free place to rest, exercise, and meet others. In dense cities, not everyone has a large home or private outdoor space. A well-designed park can support health without requiring expensive memberships or long travel. The value of parks is that they make a healthier lifestyle more accessible.
---
prompt: Why do some adults stop learning new skills? — "They are busy."
hint: Thời gian, sợ thất bại, áp lực thực tế, chốt nhỏ bước.
model: Many adults stop learning new skills because they feel busy and do not want to look inexperienced again. Work, family, and daily responsibilities can make learning feel like an extra burden. In reality, people may also fear slow progress, especially when younger people learn faster. A practical approach is to make learning smaller and more regular, rather than treating it as a big life project.
