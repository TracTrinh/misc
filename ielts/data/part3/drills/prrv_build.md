layers: POSITION | REASON | REALITY | VALUE
---
question: Do you think people should spend less time on social media?
model_POSITION: Yes, many people should reduce it, but not give it up completely.
model_REASON: Social media is useful, but it can easily consume attention.
model_REALITY: People often check short videos or updates many times a day without planning to.
model_VALUE: The goal should be healthier use, not rejecting technology entirely.
---
question: Why do some people choose to live in the countryside?
model_POSITION: There are several reasons, especially lifestyle and cost.
model_REASON: The countryside can offer more space, cleaner air, and a slower pace.
model_REALITY: After years in crowded cities, some people want quieter homes or cheaper living.
model_VALUE: It is not suitable for everyone, but it can be a better match for certain stages of life.
---
question: Are family relationships different today compared with the past?
model_POSITION: Yes, they are different in both distance and communication style.
model_REASON: Many people move away for study or work, but technology helps them stay in touch.
model_REALITY: In the past, several generations often lived closer together, while now families may be spread across cities or countries.
model_VALUE: The form of family life changes, but emotional responsibility still matters.
---
question: What are the advantages and disadvantages of tourism?
model_POSITION: Tourism can help local economies, but it can also create pressure.
model_REASON: It brings money to hotels, restaurants, transport, and small businesses.
model_REALITY: However, popular places may become crowded and expensive for local residents.
model_VALUE: Tourism is valuable when it is managed to protect both visitors' experience and local life.
---
question: How might work change in the future?
model_POSITION: Work will probably become more flexible and more technology-based.
model_REASON: Digital tools allow people to work from different locations and automate routine tasks.
model_REALITY: Many companies already use online meetings, shared documents, and AI tools in daily work.
model_VALUE: The challenge is to keep human skills such as judgment, communication, and responsibility.
---
question: What can schools do to help students become healthier?
model_POSITION: Schools can make health part of daily routines, not only a subject.
model_REASON: Students need practical habits around food, exercise, sleep, and stress.
model_REALITY: Many young people sit for long hours and use screens heavily after class.
model_VALUE: Education should help students protect their bodies as well as improve their grades.
---
question: Do you think people value old buildings enough?
model_POSITION: Not always, especially when land becomes expensive.
model_REASON: Old buildings connect people with history and give cities character.
model_REALITY: In many growing cities, older areas are replaced by apartments or shopping centers.
model_VALUE: Development is necessary, but it should not erase every visible link to the past.
---
question: How can governments encourage people to use public transport?
model_POSITION: They need to make public transport reliable, safe, and comfortable.
model_REASON: People will not change habits if buses or trains are slow and unpredictable.
model_REALITY: For many commuters, time and convenience matter more than environmental slogans.
model_VALUE: A successful policy should make the better choice also the easier choice.
