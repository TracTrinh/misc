title: 4-Line Planning
intro: Với mỗi câu hỏi, tự viết 4 dòng theo P-R-R-V, rồi mở gợi ý từng tầng để đối chiếu.
layers: POSITION | REASON | REALITY | VALUE
---
question: Do you think technology makes people less patient?
model_POSITION: Yes, in some ways.
model_REASON: Technology has trained people to expect instant results.
model_REALITY: Fast messages, online shopping, short videos one after another.
model_VALUE: Technology isn't the only problem; habits are. What matters is whether people still practise patience.
---
question: Should children learn how to manage money at school?
model_POSITION: Yes, to some extent.
model_REASON: Money habits are formed quite early.
model_REALITY: Teenagers are influenced by online shopping, ads and social media.
model_VALUE: The goal is not to make them worry, but to build simple habits like saving and comparing prices.
---
question: Is it better to grow up in a city or the countryside?
model_POSITION: It really depends on what you value more.
model_REASON: Cities offer better education and healthcare, while the countryside provides a healthier and more peaceful environment.
model_REALITY: For instance, a child in the city has access to museums and language centers, but a child in the country has more open space to play.
model_VALUE: What matters most is a supportive family environment, regardless of the location.
