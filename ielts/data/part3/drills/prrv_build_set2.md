layers: POSITION | REASON | REALITY | VALUE
---
question: Do you think public transport should be improved in cities?
model_POSITION: Yes, but it has to be improved in practical ways.
model_REASON: People will only use it more if it is reliable, safe, and easy to understand.
model_REALITY: In many cities, buses are cheaper than private transport, but they can be slow or crowded.
model_VALUE: The aim should be to give people a real choice, not to blame them for their habits.
---
question: Why do people remember some advertisements more than others?
model_POSITION: People remember advertisements that connect with emotion or real life.
model_REASON: A simple story is often easier to remember than a list of product features.
model_REALITY: Many successful ads show ordinary situations, such as family meals, work stress, or personal improvement.
model_VALUE: Good advertising is not only loud; it understands what people already care about.
---
question: Are museums less important because of the internet?
model_POSITION: No, but their role has changed.
model_REASON: The internet gives information quickly, while museums give a physical and emotional experience.
model_REALITY: Young visitors may still come if the displays are interactive and easy to follow.
model_VALUE: Tradition survives better when it learns how to communicate with modern audiences.
---
question: What are the disadvantages of online shopping?
model_POSITION: The main disadvantage is that it can encourage careless buying.
model_REASON: When payment and delivery are easy, people may skip the normal thinking process.
model_REALITY: During big sales, many people buy small items they do not truly need.
model_VALUE: Convenience is useful, but it should be balanced with self-control.
---
question: Will people read less news in the future?
model_POSITION: They may read fewer long articles, but they will not stop following news.
model_REASON: People still need updates for safety, work, money, and daily decisions.
model_REALITY: However, many now get news through short posts, videos, or notifications.
model_VALUE: The challenge is not access to news, but the ability to judge quality.
---
question: How can people manage their time better?
model_POSITION: They can manage time better by reducing small daily distractions.
model_REASON: Many people lose time not through one big mistake, but through repeated interruptions.
model_REALITY: Phones, messages, and short videos can break concentration many times a day.
model_VALUE: Good time management is less about being busy and more about protecting attention.
---
question: Should children be encouraged to have pets?
model_POSITION: Yes, if the family can care for the pet responsibly.
model_REASON: Pets can teach children patience, empathy, and routine.
model_REALITY: But in some families, adults end up doing all the work, or the pet is not treated well.
model_VALUE: A pet should be a responsibility lesson, not just a cute object.
---
question: How can schools teach teamwork more effectively?
model_POSITION: Schools should teach teamwork through structured group tasks.
model_REASON: Students need to learn roles, communication, and responsibility, not just work beside each other.
model_REALITY: In many group projects, one strong student does most of the work while others contribute little.
model_VALUE: Fair teamwork needs clear expectations and honest reflection.
