layers: POSITION | REASON | REALITY | VALUE

question: Will artificial intelligence replace human teachers in the future?
model_POSITION: I strongly believe that artificial intelligence will never completely replace human educators.
model_REASON: AI lacks emotional intelligence and the ability to provide genuine psychological support to struggling students.
model_REALITY: While software can instantly grade math tests, it cannot comfort a disappointed child or understand their personal anxieties.
model_VALUE: Technology should be treated as a powerful teaching assistant, but the human connection remains the heart of true education.

---

question: What can individuals do to combat environmental pollution?
model_POSITION: In my opinion, small lifestyle adjustments by individuals can collectively create a massive ecological impact.
model_REASON: Industrial waste follows consumer demand, so changing consumer habits forces corporations to become greener.
model_REALITY: Millions of urban citizens are now bringing cloth bags to supermarkets and sorting their plastic trash at home daily.
model_VALUE: Environmental protection is not just the responsibility of politicians; it starts with personal accountability in our daily choices.
