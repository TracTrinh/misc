layers: POSITION | REASON | REALITY | VALUE

---

question: Should schools ban students from using smartphones in classrooms?
model_POSITION: I firmly believe that a complete ban is necessary during lesson hours.
model_REASON: Smartphones are massive sources of distraction that constantly break students concentration.
model_REALITY: In many schools, teenagers secretly text under their desks or check social media notifications instead of listening to teachers.
model_VALUE: Banning phones is not about restricting freedom; it is about creating a healthy, focused space where students can actually learn.

---

question: Do you think online shopping will completely replace traditional markets?
model_POSITION: In my view, online platforms will dominate but will never fully eradicate physical markets.
model_REASON: Traditional markets offer immediate sensory verification and human interaction that websites cannot replicate.
model_REALITY: Elderly people still prefer visiting local grocery stores to select fresh ingredients and chat with familiar vendors every morning.
model_VALUE: The future is not a total digital takeover, but a co-existence where convenience meets traditional lifestyle values.

---

question: Why are people moving from rural areas to big cities?
model_POSITION: The primary driver behind this migration trend is the search for better life opportunities.
model_REASON: Metropolitan areas centralize advanced healthcare systems, prestigious education, and high-paying jobs.
model_REALITY: Thousands of young graduates leave their quiet hometowns every year to seek corporate employment in major economic hubs.
model_VALUE: While urbanization is inevitable, governments must develop rural infrastructure to balance regional growth and reduce pressure on cities.
