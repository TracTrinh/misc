title: Nới tầng VALUE (Balance)
intro: Chốt câu trưởng thành, không cực đoan (However... / What matters most is... / The goal is not to..., but to...).
---
prompt: Children use too much technology.
hint: Không cấm công nghệ, mà là cân bằng
model: However, I don't think we should completely ban screens. What matters most is teaching children how to use technology responsibly alongside outdoor activities.
---
prompt: Money doesn't bring happiness.
hint: Tiền không mua được hạnh phúc nhưng giúp cuộc sống ổn định
model: While it's true that money can't buy deep relationships, we have to admit that it provides a sense of security. The goal is not to chase money blindly, but to have enough to live comfortably.
