title: Nới tầng REALITY
intro: Đưa thực tế xã hội vào bài. Nâng từ ý cá nhân lên đời sống (In real life... / For example, many people...).
---
prompt: Technology makes people lazy.
hint: Ví dụ ngoài đời thực về việc lười vận động hoặc lười suy nghĩ
model: In real life, many young people rely completely on their smartphones for everything, from ordering food to finding directions, instead of walking or thinking for themselves.
---
prompt: Fast food is popular.
hint: Đời thực: sinh viên, người đi làm ăn nhanh vì bận
model: For example, if you look at office workers or students today, they are always rushing. They usually grab a quick burger or sandwich because they simply don't have time to cook.
