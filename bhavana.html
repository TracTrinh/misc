iv.

References

Books, commentaries, dictionaries, and learning resources compiled for the Theravāda practitioner.

A Suggested Path

From text to cultivation

A progression that mirrors the classical threefold training.

Pariyatti
Learn the text — read, listen, understand the words and their meaning in context.
Paṭipatti
Practice regularly — chant, memorize, work through exercises with attention.
Paṭivedha
Penetrate the meaning — let the Dhamma enter the mind through sustained cultivation.
Bhāvanā
Cultivate the mind — the fruit of the three preceding stages, and the purpose of the path.
